Selected work

Three products in production. For each one I took the brief through research, design, build and launch.

MarinePulse

Sightings dashboard for IMMRAC, the Israel Marine Mammal Research and Assistance Center

Visit site (opens in a new tab)

Overview

An interactive map and analysis of 351 marine mammal reports from the coasts of Lebanon, Syria, Gaza and Egypt, compiled over eight months of open-source research. Every point links to its original post, with species, group size, condition and a credibility rating.

Execution & stack

I designed an 11-stage pipeline: Arabic and English queries, Apify scrapers for TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, and filters that drop aquariums, whale sharks and AI clips. A custom Claude skill reads each video frame by frame to log species, count and location, then every record is checked against the master sheet. The dashboard is plain JavaScript and Leaflet, with clustering, a density layer and live filters.

  • Claude Code skills
  • Apify
  • ScrapeCreators
  • yt-dlp
  • ffmpeg
  • Leaflet
  • JavaScript
  • GitHub Pages

Impact

Turned scattered posts from coastlines that are hard to survey in person into a structured, citable dataset, including 37 strandings and 16 monk seal reports, a species the IUCN lists as Vulnerable. The pipeline re-runs on a schedule, so the record keeps growing without manual searching.

MyCan

3D can designer and WhatsApp ordering for a custom-filled can brand

Overview

A Hebrew, right-to-left site where customers design a branded can, pick a filling and send a ready order. Ten full-screen frames take a visitor from first impression to checkout in WhatsApp.

Execution & stack

A real-time 3D can built with React Three Fiber and GSAP shows the customer's text or logo on the label as they type. Uploaded artwork goes to a private storage bucket, and the WhatsApp order carries a signed link to it, because a wa.me message can only carry text. On phones, each 3D scene loads one screen early, compiles while the browser is idle and renders only while visible.

  • TypeScript

  • React Three Fiber
  • GSAP
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Supabase

Impact

Replaced a chat-and-photos sales process with a self-serve flow. Orders reach the owner with the design, filling and artwork already attached, ready to quote. The site also ships with an Israeli accessibility statement that lists only what the site actually does.

Esther Gomelle

Hebrew, booking-focused website for a two-branch aesthetic clinic

Overview

A calm, editorial Hebrew site for a cosmetician with studios in Holon and Herzliya, built to turn social media interest into booked consultations. It leads with proof: before-and-after results grouped by skin concern, a full treatment menu and a video wall of client recommendations.

Execution & stack

I structured the right-to-left page as a conversion path: story, a studio gallery, 22 treatments in three categories, before-and-after pairs for five skin concerns, a video recommendations wall and partner brands. Every treatment has its own WhatsApp button that opens a chat already naming that treatment, and a bottom dock keeps navigation one tap away on phones, where most visitors arrive.

  • Tailwind CSS
  • shadcn/ui
  • Lovable

Impact

Gave the clinic a web presence that matches the in-studio experience and removed the friction between interest and booking. From any of the 22 treatments, a visitor is one tap from a WhatsApp conversation that already names what they want.

Automations and AI workflows

The less visible half of my work: pipelines that collect, read and route social media at a volume no analyst could cover by hand.

Client confidential

Narrative monitoring pipeline

Built for a risk-intelligence consultancy that tracks antisemitic narratives online. Analysts used to find, download, watch and log every video by hand. Now they start from a reviewed, prioritized queue.

  1. Collect

    Public posts and videos from six platforms, pulled on a schedule.

    Apify · ScrapeCreators

  2. Orchestrate

    Scenarios trigger runs and move batches between tools.

    Make.com

  3. Process

    Each video is downloaded, split into frames and transcribed.

    ffmpeg · faster-whisper

  4. Enrich

    A two-sentence summary plus topic, country, speaker and priority.

    Claude

  5. Review

    An analyst approves or overrides each row and records why.

    Google Sheets

  6. Route

    Approved items reach the right regional team every five minutes.

    Python worker

What changed

Research time per item dropped sharply, coverage became consistent across platforms and languages, and analyst hours moved from collection to judgment and reporting.

Next: a classifier that flags AI-generated video, now training on analyst labels.

Safeguards by design

  • Open sources only

    Built on publicly available posts, the standard for open-source intelligence work.

  • An analyst decides

    The model drafts and a person approves. Every decision carries a recorded reason.

  • Zero media footprint

    Downloaded video and audio are deleted after analysis. Only the structured record is kept.
